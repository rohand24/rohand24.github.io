## Research Appointments

**Graduate Researcher, Department of Biomedical Engineering** | Georgia Tech and Emory University | 2022-Present

- Developed multimodal AI models for heart transplant rejection surveillance using endomyocardial biopsy histopathology and ECG foundation-model embeddings. A gated fusion model improved C-index over EMB-only modeling (0.66 vs. 0.61) in a held-out cohort and demonstrated significant Kaplan-Meier risk separation.
- Built longitudinal ECG foundation-model approaches for non-invasive cardiac allograft rejection surveillance, achieving 15.1% biopsy reduction at 95.9% sensitivity compared with 6.9% for a clinical baseline.
- Developed retinal vascular AI biomarkers for cardiovascular risk prediction in chronic kidney disease, outperforming the AHA-PREVENT calculator in the CARE-CKD cohort (C-index 0.70 vs. 0.65; n~800).
- Designed STAR-Echo, a spatiotemporal echocardiography biomarker combining transformer-based video representations and radiomics for MACE prognosis in CKD patients (AUC 0.71).
- Collaborated with cardiology, nephrology, ophthalmology, pathology, and transplant medicine teams on study design, model validation, statistical analysis, and interpretation of clinically deployable AI tools.

**Research Assistant, Center for Computational Imaging and Personalized Diagnostics** | Case Western Reserve University | 2020-2022

- Developed deep learning and radiomics pipelines for opportunistic hepatic steatosis assessment from low-dose coronary calcium CT.
- Co-invented DeHFT/LARI approaches for automated liver fat quantification from routinely acquired CT, later validated across multi-site cardiovascular outcomes cohorts and associated with an allowed US patent.
- Built coronary CT analysis methods for patient-specific coronary artery disease characterization and radiomic feature extraction.

**Research Assistant, Machine Intelligence Laboratory** | Rochester Institute of Technology | 2016-2018

- Developed graph neural network, object detection, and computer vision methods for point cloud feature extraction, traffic sign recognition, philately understanding, and medical image classification.
- Collaborated with VisualDx Inc. on machine learning methods for clinical skin lesion diagnosis.
